1. Purpose
This manual establishes the standard operating procedures (SOP) for regional store
inventory replenishment, promotional display audits, and approval authority matrices
across all retail business units.

2. Approval Authority Matrix by Transaction Tier
- Tier 1 (Under KRW 5,000,000)       : Store Manager / Branch Operations Lead
- Tier 2 (KRW 5,000,000 - 30,000,000): Regional Operations Director
- Tier 3 (Over KRW 30,000,000)       : Division Executive & Finance Committee

3. AI Assistant & NotebookLM Usage Guidelines
- Employees may freely upload and analyze general operational manuals, public
  merchandising guidelines, and non-sensitive training materials in both Gemini
  Enterprise Assistant Chat and NotebookLM Enterprise.
- Documents containing zero Sensitive Data Protection (SDP) InfoType matches
  receive an immediate ALLOW verdict from the Cloud DLP Content Policy and are
  indexed into the Notebook workspace without restriction.
